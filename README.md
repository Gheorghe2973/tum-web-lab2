# MedCore — Medical Equipment Landing Page

A landing page for **MedCore**, a medical equipment company offering patient monitoring, imaging, ventilators, lab diagnostics, and infusion systems.

**Live demo:** https://gheorghe2973.github.io/tum-web-lab2/

---

## Preview

### Hero — front page

The hero section features a full-screen image slider with a headline, subtitle, and a call-to-action button. The navigation bar includes a mega-dropdown for product categories and collapses into a hamburger menu on mobile.

### Products & Solutions

A product grid showcasing the core equipment catalogue with images, names, and short descriptions.
